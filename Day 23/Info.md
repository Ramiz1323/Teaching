## Authentication
- It identifies the user and verifies the user is who they claim to be.
- It is a way to verify the identity of a user.

## Authorization
- It determines what a user is allowed to do.
- It is a way to control access to resources.

## Validation
- It checks that the data being sent is valid.
- It is a way to ensure that the data being sent is correct and complete.

## Verification
- It checks that the data being sent is from a trusted source.
- It is a way to ensure that the data being sent is from a trusted source.
